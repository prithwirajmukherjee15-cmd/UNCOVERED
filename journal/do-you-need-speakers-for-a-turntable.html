Getting started

Do you need speakers for a turntable?

Turntables, preamps and speakers, explained in plain words. Plus the simplest setup that just works.

By the Uncovered team6 min read28 October 2026
Short answer

Yes, in almost every case. A turntable only reads the record; it doesn't amplify the sound. You need speakers or headphones, plus amplification. The simplest setup is a turntable with a built-in phono preamp plugged into a pair of powered speakers. Only all-in-one record players have speakers built in.

How a turntable makes sound

The stylus follows the groove and the cartridge turns those tiny movements into an electrical signal. That signal is very weak, so it goes through a phono preamp (also called a phono stage), which boosts it and applies the equalisation every record needs. Then an amplifier drives the speakers. Some of these parts can be built into the same box, but all of them have to be there.

Four ways to hear your records

SetupWhat you needBest for
Powered speakersTurntable with built-in preamp + powered (active) speakersThe simplest setup. Two cables and you're playing.
Hi-fi systemTurntable, phono stage (if not built in), amplifier or receiver, passive speakersUpgrading one part at a time
BluetoothBluetooth turntable + Bluetooth speaker or headphonesConvenience. Wireless audio is compressed, so you lose a little detail.
HeadphonesTurntable with preamp + headphone amplifier, or a phono stage with headphone outputLate nights and small flats

Powered or passive speakers?

Powered speakers have an amplifier built in, so they plug straight into a turntable with a preamp. Passive speakers need a separate amplifier or receiver. Passive systems take more space and money but let you upgrade each part over time.

What about all-in-one record players?

Record players with built-in speakers are compact and cheap, but their speakers are small and sit right next to the stylus, which can cause vibration and skipping. Many also use heavy tonearms. Read are cheap record players bad for vinyl? before you buy one.

Questions people also ask

Can I plug a turntable into any speaker?
Only into powered speakers, and only if the turntable has a built-in preamp (or you add a phono stage). Without a preamp the sound will be very quiet and thin.
Can I connect a turntable to a soundbar?
Yes, if the soundbar has an analogue or optical input and your turntable has a built-in preamp or a separate phono stage feeding it.
Do your turntables have a built-in preamp?
Each product page says so. The Audio-Technica AT-LP60 has one built in; our Rega, Technics and Pro-Ject decks need a separate phono stage or an amplifier with a phono input.
