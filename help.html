How we grade

Every second-hand record is inspected under a bright light, cleaned, and played on our test turntable. We grade the record and the sleeve separately, using the standard scale collectors use worldwide.

GradeWhat it means at Uncovered
Mint (M)Unplayed, usually still sealed. We rarely use it.
Near Mint (NM)Looks and plays like new. No audible surface noise.
Very Good Plus (VG+)Light signs of play. Faint noise in quiet passages at most.
Very Good (VG)Audible surface noise in quiet parts, but plays through without skips.
Good (G)Constant noise. We only sell G copies of rare records, and say so.

Not happy with a used record's grade? Send it back within 30 days for a full refund.

NFC trouble­shooting

Nothing happens when you tap the sleeve? Work through these in order:

  1. Unlock your phone. Most phones only read NFC when the screen is on and unlocked.
  2. Turn NFC on (Android). Settings → Connected devices (or Connections) → NFC. On iPhone XS and newer it's always on.
  3. Find the sweet spot. iPhone: the top edge. Android: the middle of the back. Hold it on the ◉ mark in the top-right corner of the sleeve for two seconds.
  4. Take off thick cases. Metal, magnetic or wallet cases can block the signal.
  5. Use the sleeve code. Still nothing? Enter the code printed on the inner sleeve on our unlock page. It opens the same hub.

If the code doesn't work either, contact us with your order number and the album name. We'll fix it, or send a replacement sleeve.

FAQ

How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery takes 3–5 working days and is free on orders over €60 (otherwise €4.90). Express takes 1–2 working days for €9.90.
When do pre-orders ship?
So they arrive on or around release day. Pre-order sleeves often unlock bonus content, like demo versions.
Can I return a record?
Yes. Unopened items within 30 days for a full refund. Faulty or damaged records are replaced free of charge.
Do I need special equipment to play your records?
Any turntable that plays 33⅓ RPM works. You'll also need speakers and, if your turntable has no built-in preamp, a phono stage. See do you need speakers for a turntable?
Does the hub work without an internet connection?
No. The sleeve opens a web page, so your phone needs data or Wi-Fi.
Can a friend tap my sleeve?
Of course. The sleeve opens the hub on any phone. It's a nice thing to share.
Do you sell second-hand records?
Yes. Every used record is cleaned, played and graded by hand, with separate grades for the record and the sleeve. See how we grade, or browse used & graded.
Do you rent turntables?
Not at the moment. Our pre-owned turntables are a good way to start for less, and each one is serviced and covered by a 6-month warranty.
