Tap the sleeve. Uncover the record.

Every Uncovered record comes in a connected sleeve. It looks and feels like any other sleeve, but hold your phone to it and the album opens up: the story behind it, an interview, lyrics and a live session. No app, no account, no battery.

  1. Step 01

    Choose your record

    Browse by artist or genre. Each product page lists exactly what's inside that album's sleeve.

  2. Step 02

    Play it like any record

    It's real 180g vinyl, made to be played. The chip is sealed inside the sleeve card, never near your stylus.

  3. Step 03

    Tap the corner mark

    Hold your phone to the ◉ mark on the top-right corner of the front sleeve for a second. A notification appears. Tap it.

  4. Step 04

    Explore the hub

    The album's hub opens in your browser: album story, interview, lyrics and live session. Come back as often as you like.

Troubleshooting

iPhone

  1. Works on iPhone XS and newer. NFC reading is always on; no setting needed.
  2. Unlock your phone and hold the top edge near the ◉ mark on the sleeve.
  3. Tap the notification that appears. The hub opens in Safari.

Android

  1. Check NFC is switched on: Settings → Connected devices (or Connections) → NFC.
  2. Unlock your phone and hold the middle of the back against the ◉ mark.
  3. The hub opens in your browser. If it asks, choose your browser.

No NFC? No problem.

Enter your sleeve code

Every sleeve has a code printed on the inner sleeve, next to the barcode. It opens the same hub.

Quick answers

Good questions

All FAQs
Do I need an app?
No. Tapping the sleeve opens the album's hub in your phone's web browser. Nothing to install.
Does the chip need a battery?
No. NFC chips are passive: your phone powers them for the split second it reads them, so the sleeve never needs charging.
Will the chip affect my record or turntable?
No. It sits inside the outer sleeve card and only wakes up when a phone is held right next to it.
What if my phone doesn't have NFC?
Use the code printed on the inner sleeve with the form above. It opens the same hub.
Can I lose access to the content?
No. The content belongs to your record. Tap the sleeve again whenever you like, on any phone.
