We dig through the crates so you don't have to.

Uncovered is a record shop for people who'd rather listen than scroll. We stock new releases, preorders and second-hand records, and every one is checked by hand before it ships. Each record leaves in an Uncovered sleeve: tap it with your phone and the record's page opens, with the tracklist, the credits and where to listen.

  1. Step 01 · Dig

    We dig

    New pressings every Friday, preorders before they sell out, and second-hand records from collections, fairs and flea markets.

  2. Step 02 · Check

    We check every record

    Used records are cleaned, played and graded separately for the record and the sleeve. Turntables are serviced and tested at every speed.

  3. Step 03 · Ship

    It ships in an Uncovered sleeve

    Rigid mailer, corner protectors, a fresh inner sleeve, and our outer sleeve with a small NFC tag inside.

  4. Step 04 · Tap

    Tap the sleeve

    Hold your phone to the sleeve. The record's page opens in your browser: our notes, the tracklist, the credits and where to listen.

Troubleshooting

iPhone

  1. Works on iPhone XS and newer. NFC reading is always on.
  2. Unlock your phone and hold the top edge near the corner mark on the sleeve.
  3. Tap the notification that appears. The page opens in Safari.

Android

  1. Check NFC is on: Settings → Connected devices (or Connections) → NFC.
  2. Unlock your phone and hold the middle of the back against the corner mark.
  3. The page opens in your browser.

No NFC? No problem.

Enter your sleeve code

Every Uncovered sleeve has a short code printed on the back, starting with UNC-. It opens the same page.

Good questions

All FAQs
Do I need an app?
No. Tapping the sleeve opens the record's page in your phone's browser.
Does the tag need a battery?
No. NFC tags are passive: your phone powers them for the split second it reads them.
How do you grade used records?
We inspect each record under light and play it, then grade the record and the sleeve separately on the standard scale from Mint to Poor. Read our grading guide.
What if my phone doesn't have NFC?
Use the code printed on the sleeve with the form above.
