Vinyl culture

Vinyl, more than a record:
an experience to rediscover

A symbol of an era and an object of passion, vinyl is making a strong comeback.
What if Uncovered helped you (re)discover this unique world?

UBy Uncovered|Published 15 November 2024

In recent years, vinyl has made a remarkable return to our homes. More than just a music format, it offers an authentic experience, a warm sound and a direct connection with the music. At Uncovered, we have chosen to celebrate this passion with a platform designed for music lovers, from newcomers to collectors.

A return to the roots, only better

In an age dominated by digital, vinyl offers a pause. It invites you to take your time, to listen differently and to feel the music fully. This is the philosophy we stand for at Uncovered: giving vinyl back its prestige while adapting it to the way we listen today.

A concept designed for enthusiasts

On our site, you can buy or rent a wide selection of records, unlock exclusive content through our QR codes and explore our recommendations. Whether you are looking for a classic or a new release, Uncovered is your guide to the world of vinyl.
