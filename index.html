Our story

We dig through the crates so you don't have to.

Dig

New pressings every Friday, preorders before they sell out, and second-hand records from collections, fairs and flea markets.

Check

Used records are cleaned, played and graded separately for record and sleeve. Turntables are serviced and tested at every speed.

Ship

Rigid mailers, corner protectors and a fresh inner sleeve. Free delivery over €60, 30-day returns.

Tap

Every record ships in an Uncovered sleeve with an NFC tag: tap it with your phone to see the tracklist, credits and where to listen.
