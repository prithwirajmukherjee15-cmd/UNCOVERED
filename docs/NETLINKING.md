# Uncovered — netlinking partners

Our best backlinks will come from vinyl events, music media and record communities: sites Google already sees as relevant to music, which also send us real buyers. Each partner comes with a concrete reason to link to Uncovered.

| Partner | Type | Link idea (what we offer → what we get) | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paris Loves Vinyl | Largest record convention in France (Paris) + its Paris Vinyl Sales flea market | Rent a stand or sponsor a DJ slot → listed on the exhibitors page with a link | High |
| Disquaire Day | French edition of Record Store Day, every April (18 April in 2026) | Write a guide to the exclusive releases, offer a giveaway → link from partner pages and event round-ups | High |
| Salon du Vinyle de Bourg-la-Reine | Local record fair, 22 November 2026, about 50 exhibitors | Take a stand or cover the event on our blog → link from the tourist office's event page | Medium |
| Sortir à Paris, Le Bonbon, Time Out Paris | City guides that cover every vinyl event | Send a press release when we launch or run an event (pop-up, listening party) → article mentioning us | High |
| Rock & Folk, Les Inrocks | Music and culture magazines | Pitch a story angle ("students launch a vinyl shop"), offer data from our sales (most-searched records) → quote with a link | Medium (hard to get) |
| Fnac L'Éclaireur | Editorial site of Fnac, publishes vinyl collecting guides | Offer expert tips (grading, storage) for their guides → cited as a source | Low |
| Discogs | World's biggest vinyl database and marketplace | Open an Uncovered seller profile with a link to our site; contribute release data | High (easy) |
| Turntable and hi-fi brands (Pro-Ject, Audio-Technica) and local hi-fi shops | Equipment sellers whose customers need records | Cross-promotion: "records to test your new turntable" page ↔ link to their products | Medium |
| Independent record labels and local artists | Music makers | Sell their pressings, write an artist interview → they link to their records on our shop | High |
| Radio stations (FIP, Radio Nova, student and local radios) | Music media | Sponsor a show segment or a vinyl giveaway → link on the show page | Medium |
| Vinyl creators on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube | People / influencers | Send a record to review, offer a discount code → link in the description or bio | High |
| Music blogs and forums (collectors' blogs, Reddit r/vinyl, audiophile forums) | Communities | Guest articles ("How to grade a used record"), answer questions with real help → contextual links | Medium |
| Our school, student union and alumni network | Institutional | Project page on the school site, student association partnerships → trusted .fr / .edu-style link | High (easy) |

## Pages on the site that support these links

- **Grading guide** (`help.html#grading`) and **"Is it worth buying used vinyl?"** (`journal/`) → for Fnac L'Éclaireur, forums and guest articles.
- **Last releases** (`new-releases.html`) and **release alerts** → for Disquaire Day guides and city-guide coverage.
- **Record players** (`shop.html?cat=players`) → for hi-fi brand and shop cross-promotion.
- The footer links to our Discogs and Instagram profiles (update the URLs once the accounts exist).
